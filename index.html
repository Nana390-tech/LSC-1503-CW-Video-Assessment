🔥 Engage — Would you keep watching?

Every good video wins the viewer in the first 10 seconds. Let's see why.

🎥 Extend — Plan your own video

Now you are the director. Build your title slide, jot down rough ideas, then write your notes and time yourself.

⏱ Suggested time: 45 min · Individual. Start with the ideas board (about 10 min, key words only — students who made a My First Ideas card can import it). Then students draft notes (not a word-for-word script to read). Remind them: one or two LPAs, one study skill, own words only. In the last 10 minutes, pairs rehearse Part 1 and Part 4 aloud with the timer. Students save the JSON backup to continue at home.
